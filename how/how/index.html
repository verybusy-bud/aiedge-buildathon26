A DIFFERENT KIND OF SOCIAL FEED

More context.
Less certainty theater.

Fieldnote is designed to show evidence and uncertainty together. It doesn’t decide what you should believe.

01 / ACCOUNT

Know what login verifies.

Email verification shows control of an email account. It doesn’t prove identity, presence, or truth. Public display names can be pseudonyms.

02 / CAPTURE

Keep the original intact.

Evidence is captured in the app, hashed, and timestamped when our server receives it. That helps detect later changes; it can’t prove the scene wasn’t staged.

03 / CORROBORATION

Separate witnesses, separate sources.

Reports are assessed per event. Reposts or accounts that may share one source aren’t counted as independent confirmations.

04 / PRIVACY

Share less by default.

Precise location and account details stay private. Public reports use an approximate location and a display name.

A report can be unresolved. That is more honest than a guess.
